회사에서 쓰는 표현
Let's take this offline
이 얘기는 따로 하자 (회의 중 길어지는 논의를 별도로 이어가고 싶을 때)
AThe design debate is getting long.
BLet's take this offline and report back.
Can you give me a heads-up next time?
다음엔 미리 알려 줄래요? (사전 공유가 없어 당황했을 때)
AI changed the API response format this morning.
BCan you give me a heads-up next time?
We're running out of runway
시간(자금)이 얼마 안 남았어요 (일정이나 예산 여유가 부족할 때)
ACan we add one more feature?
BWe're running out of runway, so let's focus.
Let's not reinvent the wheel
바퀴를 새로 만들지 말자 (이미 있는 해결책을 쓰자고 할 때)
AShould we build our own login system?
BLet's not reinvent the wheel. Use an existing library.
I'll ping you when it's deployed
배포되면 알려 줄게요 (작업이 끝나면 메시지로 알려 주겠다고 할 때)
AIs the hotfix live yet?
BNot yet. I'll ping you when it's deployed.
일상 회화 표현
It's a piece of cake
식은 죽 먹기예요 (아주 쉬운 일이라고 말할 때)
AWas the exam hard?
BNot at all. It was a piece of cake.
I'm running on fumes
기력이 바닥났어요 (피곤해서 거의 에너지가 없을 때)
AYou look exhausted today.
BYeah, I'm running on fumes after the late shift.
Let's play it by ear
그때 상황 봐서 정하자 (미리 계획하지 않고 즉흥적으로 할 때)
AWhat should we do this weekend?
BLet's play it by ear.
That rings a bell
어디서 들어 본 것 같아요 (기억이 어렴풋이 날 때)
ADo you know a guy named Daniel Kim?
BThat rings a bell. Did we meet him at the party?
I owe you one

AI picked up your package this morning.
BThanks so much, I owe you one.
