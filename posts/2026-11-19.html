회사 표현 5개 일상 회화 표현 5개
회사에서 쓰는 표현
Let's hash out the details
세부 사항을 끝까지 논의해서 정하자 (큰 방향은 정했고, 남은 구체적인 내용을 함께 앉아서 결정할 때)
AThe approach looks right, but I still don't know who writes the migration script.
BLet's hash out the details in a thirty-minute call. We'll leave with names and dates.
Can we put a kill switch on that?
그 기능에 긴급 차단 스위치를 달 수 있을까요? (문제가 생기면 배포를 되돌리지 않고 즉시 끌 수 있는 장치를 요청할 때)
AThe new recommendation engine goes live on Monday. If it misbehaves, we'd have to redeploy.
BCan we put a kill switch on that? One toggle, and traffic goes back to the old logic.
This endpoint keeps timing out
이 엔드포인트가 계속 응답 시간을 초과해요 (API 호출이 정해진 시간 안에 끝나지 않고 끊길 때)
AThree customers reported the export button does nothing after they click it.
BThis endpoint keeps timing out. The query runs for forty seconds and the gateway gives up at thirty.
Let's split this into two PRs
이건 PR 두 개로 나누자 (한 번에 올린 변경이 너무 커서 리뷰하기 어려울 때 작게 쪼개자고 할 때)
AMy branch has the refactor and the new feature together. It's about nine hundred lines.
BLet's split this into two PRs. The refactor can merge today, and the feature gets a real review.
Let's benchmark it before we optimize
최적화하기 전에 먼저 성능을 측정해 보자 (느린 원인을 추측하지 말고 숫자로 확인한 다음 고치자고 할 때)
AI think the slowness is coming from the image resizing, so I'll rewrite that part first.
BLet's benchmark it before we optimize. Last time the real cost turned out to be in the database.
일상 회화 표현
It's not my call
그건 내가 결정할 일이 아니야 (결정 권한이 자신에게 없다고 정중히 밝힐 때. 책임을 피하는 느낌 없이 자연스럽게 쓰임)
ACan you just let us start two weeks early? You're the one running the project.
BIt's not my call. The budget has to be approved upstairs, and I can only ask.
Let's not get ahead of ourselves
너무 앞서가지 말자 (아직 확정되지 않은 일을 벌써 다 된 것처럼 이야기할 때 속도를 늦추자고 할 때)
AIf we get this contract, we should hire two more designers and move to a bigger office.
BLet's not get ahead of ourselves. They haven't even sent the paperwork yet.
I'm all over the place today
오늘 정신이 좀 없어 (여러 일이 겹쳐서 집중이 안 되고 어수선한 상태를 말할 때)
AYou left your coffee in the meeting room and your badge at the front desk.
BI'm all over the place today. I barely slept, and I've been running since six.
That's a hard pass for me
난 그건 됐어 / 사양할게 (제안을 분명하게, 하지만 가볍고 농담처럼 거절할 때)
AA few of us are going skydiving on Saturday. There's one spot left.
BThat's a hard pass for me. I'll be the one holding the bags and taking photos.
You're in for a treat
너 정말 기대해도 돼 (상대가 아주 좋아할 만한 것이 기다리고 있다고 미리 알려줄 때)
AI've never had my mom's braised short ribs. Everyone keeps telling me about them.
BYou're in for a treat. She starts cooking the night before, and the house smells amazing.
