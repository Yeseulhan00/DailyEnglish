회사 표현 5개 일상 회화 표현 5개
회사에서 쓰는 표현
Let's kick the tires on this
한번 시험 삼아 써보자 (새로운 도구나 기능을 정식 도입하기 전에 가볍게 테스트해볼 때)
AThe vendor says this new monitoring tool can replace our whole stack.
BLet's kick the tires on this with one small service before we commit.
It's spaghetti code
코드가 완전 스파게티야 (얽히고설켜서 흐름을 따라가기 어려운 코드를 가리킬 때)
AI've been staring at this module for an hour and I still can't tell what it does.
BYeah, it's spaghetti code, half the team is afraid to touch it.
Can we get a temperature check on this?
다들 이거 어떻게 생각하는지 가볍게 확인해볼까? (정식으로 결정하기 전에 팀 분위기나 의견을 살짝 파악하고 싶을 때)
ABefore we schedule the formal vote, I want to know where people stand.
BCan we get a temperature check on this in the team channel first?
Let's put this on the roadmap
이건 로드맵에 올려두자 (지금 당장은 아니지만 앞으로 진행할 계획으로 등록해둘 때)
AA lot of customers keep asking for dark mode, but we can't do it this quarter.
BLet's put this on the roadmap for next quarter so it doesn't get forgotten.
Can we get some runway on this?
이거 처리할 시간을 좀 더 확보할 수 있을까? (마감이 촉박한 작업에 여유 시간이 필요할 때)
AThe migration is way more complicated than we scoped, and the deadline is Friday.
BCan we get some runway on this? I'd rather do it right than rush it.
일상 회화 표현
Let's bite the bullet
이제 그냥 마음먹고 해버리자 (어렵거나 하기 싫지만 피할 수 없는 일을 받아들이고 실행할 때)
AI really don't want to go to the dentist, but this toothache isn't going away.
BLet's bite the bullet and just book the appointment today.
I'm counting my chickens before they hatch
김칫국부터 마시는 거지 (아직 확실하지 않은 일을 벌써 다 된 것처럼 기대할 때)
AI already told my parents I'm getting the promotion next month.
BCareful, you might be counting your chickens before they hatch.
Let's cross that bridge when we come to it
그건 그때 가서 생각하자 (아직 일어나지 않은 문제를 미리부터 걱정하지 않으려 할 때)
AWhat if it rains on the day of the picnic?
BLet's cross that bridge when we come to it, the forecast looks fine for now.
It's a dime a dozen
그런 건 널리고 널렸어 (특별할 것 없이 아주 흔하게 볼 수 있는 것을 말할 때)
AThis coffee shop looks so unique, I feel like I found a hidden gem.
BHonestly, places like this are a dime a dozen around here.
Let's get down to business
이제 본론으로 들어가자 (가벼운 이야기를 마치고 진짜 중요한 얘기를 시작할 때)
AIt's so nice catching up, how has your week been?
BIt has, but let's get down to business, we only have the room for 20 minutes.
