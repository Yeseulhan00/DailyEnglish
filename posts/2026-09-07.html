회사 표현 5개 일상 회화 표현 5개
회사에서 쓰는 표현
It's low-hanging fruit
쉽게 처리할 수 있는 일이야 (큰 노력 없이 빠르게 성과를 낼 수 있는 작업을 가리킬 때)
AWe have a long list of improvements but limited time this sprint.
BLet's start with this one, it's low-hanging fruit and we can knock it out today.
Can we get a sign-off on this?
이거 승인 받을 수 있을까? (변경 사항이나 계획을 진행하기 전에 책임자의 최종 승인을 요청할 때)
AThe design mockups are finalized and ready to hand off to engineering.
BGreat, can we get a sign-off on this from the product lead before we start building?
Let's fast-track this
이거 빠르게 처리하자 (일반적인 절차보다 더 급하게, 우선적으로 진행하자고 할 때)
AThe client says they need this feature by next Friday.
BOkay, let's fast-track this and pull in an extra engineer to help.
This test is flaky
이 테스트는 가끔 실패해 (같은 코드인데도 테스트가 통과했다 실패했다 불안정하게 동작할 때)
AThe CI pipeline failed again but nothing in the code changed.
BIgnore it, this test is flaky, just rerun the pipeline and it should pass.
Let's unblock this
이거 막힌 걸 풀어주자 (다른 사람이나 다음 작업이 진행되지 못하게 막고 있는 문제를 먼저 해결할 때)
AThe frontend team can't move forward until the API is ready.
BLet's unblock this today, I'll finish the endpoint by this afternoon.
일상 회화 표현
Better late than never
늦더라도 안 하는 것보다는 나아 (약속이나 할 일을 늦게라도 하게 되었을 때 위안 삼아 하는 말)
AI'm sorry I didn't reply to your message until now.
BBetter late than never, I'm just glad you finally got back to me.
I'm getting cold feet
갑자기 겁이 나서 망설여져 (중요한 결정을 앞두고 불안하고 자신이 없어질 때)
AAre you still going to sign up for the marathon?
BHonestly, I'm getting cold feet, I'm not sure I've trained enough.
It's raining cats and dogs
비가 억수같이 쏟아져 (비가 아주 세차게 많이 내릴 때)
AShould we walk to the restaurant or take a cab?
BLet's take a cab, it's raining cats and dogs out there.
Break a leg
행운을 빌어 (공연, 시험, 발표 등을 앞둔 사람을 격려하며 하는 말)
AI'm so nervous about my presentation this afternoon.
BYou'll do great, just break a leg out there.
I'm on a roll
요즘 일이 술술 잘 풀려 (연달아 좋은 성과를 내고 있어서 그 흐름을 계속 타고 있을 때)
AYou've finished three chapters of your book this week already.
BI know, I'm on a roll, I don't want to stop while it's going well.
