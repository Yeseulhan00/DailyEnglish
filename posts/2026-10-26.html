What's the biggest pain point for users?
사용자의 가장 큰 불편 사항이 뭐예요? (제품의 문제점을 파악할 때)
AWe have a lot of feedback to review.
BWhat's the biggest pain point for users?
That's just a nitpick, feel free to ignore it
사소한 지적이니 무시해도 돼요 (코드 리뷰에서 가벼운 의견을 낼 때)
AYou left a comment on my pull request?
BThat's just a nitpick, feel free to ignore it.
I'll skip stand-up today
오늘 스탠드업(아침 짧은 회의)은 빠질게요 (정례 회의에 불참할 때)

We need to avoid feature creep
기능이 계속 불어나는 건 피해야 해요 (요구사항이 자꾸 추가될 때)
AThe client wants three more features.
BWe need to avoid feature creep, or we'll miss the deadline.
Let's wrap up in five minutes
5분 안에 마무리합시다 (회의를 끝내려 할 때)
AWe're running out of time.
BLet's wrap up in five minutes and assign the tasks.

BI'm running on fumes. I really need a break.
That costs an arm and a leg
그건 엄청 비싸요 (값이 터무니없이 비쌀 때)
AWhy don't you buy a house in Seoul?
BIt costs an arm and a leg!
Everything is under control
모든 게 잘 관리되고 있어요 (걱정하는 사람을 안심시킬 때)

We're back to square one
원점으로 돌아왔어요 (애쓴 일이 처음부터 다시일 때)
AThe file was deleted by mistake.
BOh no, we're back to square one.
Hold your horses

ALet's book the tickets right now!
BHold your horses. We haven't checked the dates yet.
