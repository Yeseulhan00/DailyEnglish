회사에서 쓰는 표현
We're boiling the ocean here
지금 우리는 바다를 끓이려 하고 있어요 (한 번에 모든 걸 다 해결하려다 범위가 끝없이 커져서 아무것도 못 끝내는 상황을 지적할 때)
AThe migration plan now covers every service we own.
BWe're boiling the ocean here. Let's move one service first and learn from it.
Let's stop the bleeding first
일단 급한 불부터 끄죠 (근본 원인을 찾기 전에 피해가 더 커지는 것을 먼저 막자고 할 때)
AShould we rewrite the retry logic before the postmortem?
BLet's stop the bleeding first. Turn off the feature flag, then we debug properly.
We're building the plane while flying it
날면서 비행기를 조립하는 중이에요 (이미 서비스가 돌아가는데 설계와 구조를 동시에 만들어 가는, 불안하지만 흔한 상황을 말할 때)
AThere's no spec for this API yet, but customers are already calling it.
BI know, we're building the plane while flying it. Let's at least freeze the request format.
That's just yak shaving
그건 결국 삽질이에요 (본래 할 일을 하려다 끝없이 딸려 나오는 준비 작업만 하고 있을 때 쓰는 개발자 표현)
AI had to upgrade Node, which broke the linter, which broke the build script.
BThat's just yak shaving. Pin the old version today and fix the real bug.
Let's not let perfect be the enemy of good
완벽을 노리다 괜찮은 걸 놓치지 말죠 (지금 충분히 쓸 만한 안이 있는데 더 완벽한 안을 기다리며 출시가 밀릴 때)
AThe dashboard still has no custom date ranges.
BLet's not let perfect be the enemy of good. Ship the weekly view and add ranges next sprint.
일상 회화 표현
Let's not count our chickens yet
아직 김칫국 마시지 말자 (아직 확정되지 않은 일을 벌써 다 된 것처럼 기뻐할 때 말리는 표현)
AI'm already looking at apartments near the new office.
BLet's not count our chickens yet. You haven't signed anything.
She threw me under the bus
그 사람이 날 팔아넘겼어 (자기가 곤란해지지 않으려고 남에게 책임을 떠넘겼을 때)
AWhy did your manager mention your name in that email?
BShe threw me under the bus. It was her decision, but she made it sound like mine.
I was biting my tongue the whole time
참느라 혼났어 (하고 싶은 말이 목까지 올라왔는데 분위기 때문에 꾹 눌러 참았을 때)
AYou were surprisingly quiet at dinner.
BI was biting my tongue the whole time. One more comment about my job and I would have snapped.
He's a stickler for details
그 사람은 디테일에 깐깐해 (규칙이나 세부 사항을 아주 엄격하게 따지는 사람을 설명할 때)
AShould I just send him a rough draft?
BHe's a stickler for details. Fix the dates and the numbers first, or he'll send it back.
It's six of one, half a dozen of the other
그게 그거야 (두 선택지의 차이가 사실상 없어서 어느 쪽을 골라도 똑같을 때)
ABus or subway? The bus is cheaper but slower.
BIt's six of one, half a dozen of the other. Just take whatever comes first.
