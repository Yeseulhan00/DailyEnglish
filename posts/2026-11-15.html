회사에서 쓰는 표현
Let's put a circuit breaker on that call
그 호출에 서킷 브레이커를 걸어두자 (장애가 난 외부 서비스를 계속 호출해서 우리까지 같이 죽는 걸 막을 때)
AThe payment API is timing out and now our whole checkout is hanging.
BLet's put a circuit breaker on that call so it fails fast instead.
Can you tail the logs?
로그 실시간으로 좀 봐줄래요? (지금 찍히는 로그를 띄워놓고 지켜봐 달라고 할 때)
AI'm about to restart the worker. Can you tail the logs?
BSure, I've got them open. Go ahead whenever you're ready.
Let's bisect to find the bad commit
이분 탐색으로 문제 커밋을 찾아보자 (언제부터 깨졌는지 모를 때 커밋을 반씩 나눠가며 범인을 찾을 때)
AIt worked two weeks ago, but there are ninety commits since then.
BLet's bisect to find the bad commit. It should only take seven tries.
This query needs an index
이 쿼리엔 인덱스가 필요해요 (DB 조회가 느릴 때, 검색용 색인을 추가하자고 할 때)
AThe report page takes eleven seconds to load now.
BThis query needs an index. It's scanning the whole table every time.
Let's set a sane default
합리적인 기본값을 정해두자 (설정을 안 건드려도 대부분 잘 돌아가게 기본값을 잡을 때)
AShould we make the timeout required so people have to think about it?
BLet's set a sane default instead. Thirty seconds works for almost everyone.
일상 회화 표현
Don't jinx it
그런 말 하면 안 돼, 김칫국 마시지 마 (잘 되고 있는데 미리 말해서 재수 없게 만들지 말라고 할 때)
AWe're going to finish way ahead of schedule at this rate.
BDon't jinx it. We still have two weeks to go.
You read my mind
내 마음을 읽었네 (상대가 내가 하려던 말이나 바라던 걸 먼저 말했을 때)
AI brought you an iced americano. You looked exhausted.
BYou read my mind. I was just about to go get one.
I'll pencil it in
일단 일정에 적어둘게 (확정은 아니지만 잠정적으로 달력에 넣어둘 때)
ADinner on Friday? I'll confirm the place later this week.
BI'll pencil it in and keep the evening free.
That's a tall order
그건 쉬운 부탁이 아니네 (요구가 과해서 들어주기 어려울 때)
ACould you learn the whole song by tomorrow night?
BThat's a tall order, but I'll see how far I get.
My hands are full
지금 손이 꽉 찼어 (이미 할 일이 많아 여유가 없을 때)
ACould you also pick up the cake on your way home?
BMy hands are full with the kids today. Can it wait until tomorrow?
